# my/knowledge/ — what the brain knows about your business

Every `.md` file in this folder is loaded, in name order, as cached context for **every**
call the brain makes, in every machine. This is the difference between a brain that reasons
and a brain that reasons *about you*.

Put here what a new employee would need on day one: what you sell and to whom, how you talk
about it, what you never say, your prices, your competitors, the three objections you hear
most and how you answer them. Plain markdown. Keep the whole folder under ~40 KB — it rides
on every call, and it is cached, so it costs little after the first send but it is not free.

The brand brief in `my/` is the *voice*. This folder is the *facts*.
